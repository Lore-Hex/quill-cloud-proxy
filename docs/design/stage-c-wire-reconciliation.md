## Lost acknowledgement: receipt-owned replay

**Fingerprint finding:** `_gateway_authorize_fingerprint` excludes both key fields, `idempotency_key`, and the receipt, but not `invocation_nonce`. Nevertheless, the real route explicitly removes the nonce from `body_dict` **before** constructing the fingerprint, in both R and P. It persists `body.invocation_nonce` separately and serializes the **stored** nonce on replay. Thus changing a nonce does not create a fingerprint conflict through the real route; calling the helper directly with a nonce would give a misleading test result. (`R:src/trusted_router/routes/internal/gateway.py:943`; `R:src/trusted_router/routes/internal/gateway.py:1343`; `R:src/trusted_router/routes/internal/gateway.py:2695`; `R:src/trusted_router/routes/internal/gateway.py:1945`; `R:src/trusted_router/routes/internal/gateway.py:3036`; `P:src/trusted_router/routes/internal/gateway.py:1007`.)

Receipt replay has stronger, separate arbitration: the pre-allocation lookup requires matching stored receipt hash; transactional replay compares ordinary fingerprints only when both receipts are absent. Receipt equality therefore does **not** imply whole-body fingerprint equality. Boot/signature, identity, policy, estimate, frozen-route and local-owner validation must stay intact with either design. (`R:src/trusted_router/storage_gcp.py:6689`; `R:src/trusted_router/storage_gcp_authorize.py:465`; `R:src/trusted_router/spend_lease_admission.py:197`; `R:src/trusted_router/routes/internal/gateway.py:2782`.)

## Streaming and decide scope

#1296's `not_streaming` refusal is valid for its initial **chat Stage C rollout**, not a permanent definition of all local admission. **R1** must introduce the independently gated non-streaming decide branch; E1 supplies its descriptor/normalization. Relax both the stream refusal **and** new/replay cap checks that unconditionally require chat/Responses plus `stage_d_reason == "ok"`. Simply removing `not_streaming`, or setting `stream=true` for decide, cannot implement A3. Stage D's heartbeat/termination eligibility remains unchanged and decide responses must not advertise Stage D eligibility. (`P:src/trusted_router/routes/internal/gateway.py:1436`; `P:src/trusted_router/routes/internal/gateway.py:1660`; `P:src/trusted_router/routes/internal/gateway.py:2945`; `R:src/trusted_router/routes/internal/gateway.py:3128`; `E:docs/design/decide-on-the-lease-path.md:326`.)

Stage C reserve still gates public output; settlement initially blocks. Decision 1 approval is required **before any decide binding**, including cold/miss/ordinary synchronous calls, because actual charges are capped at the allocation/estimate and overruns are absorbed. Issuance-only shadow must preserve ordinary billing.
